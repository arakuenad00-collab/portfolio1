X (Twitter) · Organic Growth & Content
Mshel Homes — @MshelOfficial
Inherited a dormant account at 230 followers. Rebuilt the content strategy, voice, and publishing cadence. Grew engagement, reach, and community from near-zero — entirely organic.

The brief

The account existed but had no consistent voice, no strategy, and low engagement. The challenge was to make a real estate brand feel relevant and human on a platform where Nigerian audiences are highly opinionated and quick to scroll past corporate content.

What I built

Enforced a content pillar structure — informational posts about Nigerian real estate, emotional stories tied to homeownership, and restrained promotional content. Wrote every caption to match the platform's conversational tone without losing brand credibility. Managed all replies and DMs.

Results

  • 29.6K impressions — up 293% from period start
  • 799 total engagements — up 560%
  • 374 likes — up 567%; 84 reposts — up 1K%
  • 168 profile visits — up 320%
  • Engagement rate: 2.6% — up 67%
  • Follower count grown from 230 to 465 organically
  • Single post: 778K views, 414 likes, 50 bookmarks
  • Second top post: 707K views, 457 likes, 85 bookmarks

Instagram · Organic Growth + Paid Ads
Mshel Homes Yola — Instagram
Took over a page with roughly 84 followers. No existing strategy. Built content, ran targeted ads for profile visits and follower growth, and grew the account's reach to over 119K — most of it non-follower reach.

The brief

The Yola page was new and practically empty. The goal was to establish a consistent presence for Mshel Homes in the Yola market, grow an audience from scratch, and drive awareness through both organic content and paid promotion.

What I did

Ran targeted Meta Ads focused on profile visits and follower acquisition. Built content suited to the local market. The reach data tells the real story — 118,808 of the 119K reach came from non-followers, meaning the content and ads were consistently reaching new potential buyers.

Results

  • Grew from ~84 followers to 992 lifetime followers
  • 924 new follows — up 2,200%
  • Total reach: 119K — up 14,000%
  • Non-follower reach: 118,808 (content reaching beyond existing audience)
  • 56 new messaging contacts — up 100%
