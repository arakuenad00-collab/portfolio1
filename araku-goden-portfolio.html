About

Who I am.

I'm a Social Media Manager based in Abuja, Nigeria, currently at Mshel Homes Limited — a real estate developer with properties across Abuja, Lagos, Kano, and Yola.

My strongest skill isn't scheduling or analytics, though I do both. It's writing in a brand's voice — researching the audience, understanding what actually moves them, and producing content that earns attention rather than begging for it.

I believe the compounding effect is what social media is really about. Consistent, researched, intentional stories — published long enough that the audience starts to trust you.
